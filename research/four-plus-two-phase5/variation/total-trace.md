# Why the literal input suffices on the generic total family

Let $S$ be the affine space of invariant core sextics, $T_H$ the threefold $w^3=-H$, $\rho(w)=\zeta w$ and $\sigma(c,d)=(-c,-d)$. The universal intrinsic chart is irreducible. Indeed for fixed $Q_1,Q_2,A_{\rm even},B_{\rm odd}$ the 25 equations impose independent affine linear conditions on $H$, by surjectivity onto the invariant sextics modulo $Q_2$. This makes the universal chart an affine-space bundle over the open of coefficient tuples. It dominates $S$ by the rank-25 smooth contact calculation, with generic relative dimension one. Over its generic point the quadric and cubic define a smooth geometrically integral contact. The universal contact surface over the coefficient field therefore has a function field finite over the ratio plane by the local dominance calculation. Its trace includes the conjugates over that field. The construction uses the actual localized incidence. Boundary components introduced by clearing equations require separate removal. [Intrinsic norm chart](../../../docs/03b-weighted-threefolds.md) · [Dominance at the marked contact](../quadric/finite_chart.md)

Compactify and normalize the generic parameter curve and close its contact correspondence $Z$. Over an open of $S$ the resulting proper correspondence inside the relative fiber square gives a codimension-three cycle $C=Z_*Z^*$. There is a second way to see why the total action is scalar, independent of the three-direction matrix check.

Let $W$ be the weighted ambient space minus its vertex, the total space of $\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^3}(2)$. Over point pairs in $W^2$ whose projected cores lie in distinct sign orbits, imposing that both points belong to $T_H$ gives two independent linear conditions on the coefficients of $H$. Invariant sextics separate the core orbits. For example the quadratic monomials in each of $(a,b)$ and $(c,d)$ define the two Veronese blocks. Their combined projective coordinates determine the core up to the two sign choices modulo projective scaling. Degree-three products of these quadrics give the same separation in degree six. The total fiber square above this open is an affine-space bundle. If the core orbits coincide, compatibility forces the two weighted points to differ by $\rho^k$ or $\sigma\rho^k$. Each of these six graphs over the universal hypersurface has codimension three in the total fiber square.

On the open, homotopy invariance for Chow groups and localization express a codimension-three cycle as a sum of restrictions of ambient products. Indeed the Chow group of $W^2$ comes from $\mathbf P^3\times\mathbf P^3$, and restriction onto its open is surjective. Closing the cycle from the generic parameter locus, the remaining codimension-three class is supported on the six graphs, hence is a linear combination of their fundamental classes. Restrict this description to the generic smooth fiber. The ambient products act trivially on its third cohomology. On the negative sector the graphs act through $\mathbf Q[\rho]=\mathbf Q(\zeta)$, since the invariant third cohomology is that of the quotient $\mathbf P^3$ and vanishes. The symmetry of $C$ makes its action self-adjoint for the intersection pairing. The adjoint sends $\rho$ to $\rho^{-1}$, so on this sector the scalar belongs to $\mathbf Q$. The contact pullback has rank 48 on $H^{2,1}_-$ on an open and the polarized adjoint makes the scalar nonzero. With the usual algebraic push–pull convention its value is
$$\lambda=-\frac{Z\cdot Z-Z\cdot\sigma Z}{192}.$$
Indeed the alternating trace of $C$ composed with the negative projector is half the difference of the two proper intersection numbers. Its only term is minus the trace on the 96-dimensional negative middle cohomology. For a disjoint union of parameter curves the numerator is summed componentwise. The intersection numbers and moving degree still have to be evaluated. [Independent infinitesimal scalar calculation](README.md)

Restrict the ratio base so the moving cover is finite étale and its distinct geometric sections and translates are disjoint after splitting. Delete the branch and chart boundaries, retaining an affine open $U$ of $T_H$. On a contact section at $t=+\alpha,w=\zeta^k\beta$ the logarithmic residue of the raw two-form $(\beta^2/w^2)\eta\wedge d\log((t-\alpha)/(t+\alpha))$ is $-\zeta^k\eta$ in the logarithm-first convention. The residue at $t=-\alpha$ has the opposite sign. Summing over translates gives $-6P_-P_\chi Z_*[\eta]/n$ after normalization by the moving degree $n$, for $\chi(\rho^*)=\zeta$. The connecting map and the normalization of the input form fix the overall orientation. The logarithmic residue sequence computes the class of the differentiated current on $U$, which still contains these sections. Thus for the generic total trace with input $A$ the difference from the corresponding nonzero multiple of the target class is exact on $U$. Affine algebraic de Rham then supplies a regular correcting two-form there. Its coefficients can have base, branch and chart denominators. This proves existence of such a correction to the generic traced source. An explicit rational function and a checked identity are still required. [Packet current and trace normalization](../../four-plus-two-phase4/README.md)

The raw (unnormalized) trace suffices for finding a witness. If its source is $S_0+S_1t^2+S_2t^4$, solve for a constant $c$ and a branch/base correction with source $S_0+S_1t^2+(S_2-c)t^4$. Subtract the correction from the raw flux and divide by $c$. This uses only linear equations in $c$ and the correction once the raw trace is supplied. There is at most one such $c$ on the catalogue fiber. Two would give a branch/base primitive of $t^4$ by subtraction, contradicting the slope obstruction. Uniqueness persists on a generic pencil through this point. Indeed the finite Laurent correction of a branch/base primitive retains only branch/base poles, since the initial denominator coefficients of base factors depend only on the base ratios, and $P_0$ is nonzero. Thus a branch/base primitive of $t^4$ on the pencil would specialize to one on the catalogue fiber. An exact coefficient solver reconstructs the scalar and correction for a specified rational source, denominator and numerator bounds, and rechecks the identity symbolically. On a manufactured source on the literal coupled polynomial it recovers the scalar $7/5$ and flux $(1/(1+r),0,-t/(3(1+r)^2))$. This is a positive control for the correction step. Once a generic primitive has been constructed along a line through the catalogue point, the finite Laurent specialization applies to its actual rational identity. This permits a calculation on the generic total incidence even when a special parameter curve splits. A trace on a proper subset of the fixed fiber has its own normalization and action. ## Specialize the raw trace before solving the correction

Write $P_\varepsilon=P_0+\varepsilon P_1$, $U=P_\varepsilon/P_0$, and take a generic line through the catalogue point. Suppose $q_\varepsilon$ is the raw total flux and $S_\varepsilon=D_{P_\varepsilon}(q_\varepsilon)$ its raw source. Use the same constant-field trace on both sides if descent is needed. Then
$$q_*=[\varepsilon^0]U^{-2/3}q_\varepsilon,\qquad
S_*=[\varepsilon^0]U^{-5/3}S_\varepsilon,\qquad D_{P_0}(q_*)=S_*.$$
Each extraction needs only the constant and finitely many negative Laurent coefficients. In particular the source factor has exponent $-5/3$, while the flux uses $-2/3$.

The generic compact scalar is constant on a connected spreading family by horizontality. The proportionality factor for the unnormalized current also retains the same residue and orbit conventions. Apply the coefficient formula to a generic branch/base correction with source $S_\varepsilon-c t^4$. Its specialized source is $S_*-c t^4$, and its fiber poles are confined to $P_0=0$ and the chart boundaries. Thus it suffices to compute $q_*,S_*$ and to find the branch/base correction **on the fixed catalogue polynomial**. One need not first evaluate the correction throughout the deformation family. If the source has a pole in $\varepsilon$, the resulting $S_*$ can have powers of $P_0$ in the denominator. The relative $t$-reduction must then precede the polynomial-triple solver. A partial trace on a selected set of nondivergent sheets does not justify these constant terms. ## Computing the raw trace from a finite algebra

Let $L$ be the complete localized ratio-fiber algebra and $U_i/J$ its three source entries with a common unit denominator. The three linear terms of
$$\log\frac{\det(m_J+\sum_{i=0}^2 z_i m_{U_i})}{\det m_J}$$
are the unnormalized traces $\operatorname{Tr}(m_J^{-1}m_{U_i})$ by Jacobi's determinant formula. Here $m_b$ is multiplication by $b$ in $L$. To use a single bordered Macaulay matrix, choose a quotient basis $e_j$ and a finite monomial span containing the $e_jJ$ and $e_j U_i$ and mapping onto $L$. Start with independent relation rows spanning its kernel and append the rows for $e_j(J+\sum z_iU_i)$. Its determinant divided by its constant term is the same norm ratio. Completeness of the quotient basis is essential. For each basis monomial and each generator one can check the border reduction, plus commutation of the multiplication matrices, annihilation of all localized defining equations, and independence of the evaluated basis on the unit vector.

On the complete degree-three binary control this gives $(0,0,-4iJ_XJ_Y/9)$ after normalization. Over the three distinguished coupled nodal sheets, with input $d\tau$ at $(0,1)$, the same calculation gives $(5+21v,22+2v,21+3v)$ modulo $(23,v^2-15)$. A companion-matrix calculation gives the same three values. The determinant of the full coupled algebra for $A$ is still unknown. [Border determinant checks](trace_determinant.py)

Eight nontrivial polynomial inputs give full traces that can be calculated without building the quotient. With $I=(f_1,f_2,h,g_1,g_2)$, write $G=f_1g_1+f_2g_2+h k$. Differentiating in a fixed-$G$ parameter direction gives $kDh=0$ modulo $I$, while coordinate differentiation gives $G_j=k\partial_j h$ modulo $I$. Thus $x_iG_jDh=0$ in the residue algebra. For each of the eight odd off-block Jacobian directions the canonical differential, the single-sheet source, and the full source all vanish.
