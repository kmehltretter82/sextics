# Boundary multiplicities in a count of the moving contacts

The number of sheets in the generic trace can be expressed as an intersection after resolving two base ideals. The unresolved terms include multiplicities even over smooth quadrics. On the marked slice below three such lengths are 49, 98 and 3.

Let $p$ move on the physical ratio line $\ell$, and let $Q$ be an invariant quadric with $Q(p)=0$. These pairs form the fivefold $B=\mathbf P_\ell(\ker[\mathcal O^6\to\mathcal O(2)])$ in the lines convention. Write $z$ for the point class on $\ell$ and $x$ for the relative hyperplane class. On $B$ use the bundles
$$
A=\mathcal O^6/\mathcal O(-x),\quad C=\mathcal O^{20}/\mathcal O(-x)^4,\quad
K=\ker(C\to\mathcal O(3z)),\quad W=\mathcal O^{44}/\mathcal O(-x)^{19}.
$$
They represent invariant quadrics modulo $Q$, cubics modulo $Q$, cubics vanishing at $p$, and invariant sextics modulo $Q$. Their ranks are 5, 16, 15 and 25. Put $X=\mathbf P_B(A)\times_B\mathbf P_B(K)$ with relative hyperplane classes $y,v$. This has dimension 23. For nonzero independent columns $q^3,h\sigma h$ with both coefficients nonzero in an expression for $H$, the rank-at-most-two condition on $(q^3,h\sigma h,H)$ gives the marked projective norm contacts. Each has six choices for the raw cube and square scalars.

The expected-codimension Porteous expression on $X$ has integral
$$\int_X[(1-x)^{-19}(1-3y)^{-1}(1-2v)^{-1}]_{23}=-2{,}210{,}955{,}264.$$
Here $s(A)=1-x$, $s(K)=(1-x)^4(1+3z)$, and the pushforwards of $x^4,x^5$ from $B$ are $1,2z$. The negative integer exhibits the failure of this expression as a count.

On a smooth split quadric with $\sigma(w,z)=(-w,-z)$, take a general hyperplane section $L$ through $p$ with $(\sigma L)(p)\ne0$. Then $q=L\sigma L$ and $h=L^i(\sigma L)^{3-i}$ for $i=1,2,3$ give coincident columns. Each reduced branch has dimension two for fixed $Q,p$. Linearizing the proportionality gives tangent dimensions 7, 8, 3. In particular multiplicity-one subtraction would miss contributions.

There is an exact calculation on a transverse slice. Take $L=1+w+2z+3wz$, $q_0=L\sigma L$, $q_1=1+w^2+2z^2+3wz+5w^2z^2$. Unmarked, vary $q=q_0+tq_1$. The local ring at $h=L^2\sigma L$ has Hilbert function $(1,5,14,18,10,1)$, of length 49. At $h=L^3$ the length is one. For the marking take $p=(1,-2/5)$, $q=q_0+tq_1+s(L+\sigma L)$ and impose $h(p)=0$. For $i=1,2,3$ the lengths are respectively 49, 98, 3. The verifier computes exact rational Macaulay nullspaces and certifies stabilization using an upper bound modulo 101 and Nakayama's lemma. These lengths describe the stated slices. For an actual generic count, blow up the ideal of coefficients of $q^3$ in $W$, with exceptional divisor $E_1$. The transformed line $L_1=\mathcal O(-3y+E_1)$ embeds as a subbundle in $W$. Blow up the order ideal of the image of $h\sigma h$ in $W/L_1$, obtaining $L_2=\mathcal O(-2v+E_2)$ as a subbundle there. The final quotient $V=(W/L_1)/L_2$ has rank 23 and is generated by invariant sextics. On the resulting proper irreducible graph, each point imposes 23 independent linear conditions for the section given by $H$ to vanish. Hence general $H$ avoids the boundary of dimension at most 22. On the interior, the loci where one coefficient of $H$ vanishes impose an additional condition and are likewise avoided. The marked projective degree for general $H$ is
$$\int c_{23}(V),\qquad c(V)=\frac{(1-x)^{-19}}{(1-3y+E_1)(1-2v+E_2)}.$$
To evaluate it one needs the Segre classes of both order ideals, including their special strata. Computing these and identifying the specialized components at $\kappa=5/7$ remain open.
