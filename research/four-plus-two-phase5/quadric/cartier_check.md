# Cartier on the smooth contact component at 11

Run cartier_naturality.py with the directory containing the upstream contact_series_span.py and the directory containing target_cartier.py. The check uses NumPy and the smooth_span.py and fiber_current.py helpers.

Compute the first 55 coefficients of every canonical differential by Grothendieck residue. Differentiate the raw moving cubic $A+B$ in the weighted five-variable presentation; only then substitute $u=-Q_1$. The four resulting complete-intersection generators give a degree-nine Macaulay matrix over $\mathbb F_{11}[\tau]/(\tau^{55})$. A unit minor lifts the socle functional, normalized first on one free monomial, then by $\tau_{\rm res}(\det \operatorname{Jac})=96$. The normalization includes the leading coefficient of $u$ in the original $f_1$. Multiplying by $R\,D(A+B)$ gives the canonical form for each of the 60 anti-invariant weighted degree-six monomials. This computation uses no Cartier matrix. Comparing its target form to the independently normalized obstruction row gives a common unit for all 60 forms. Its coefficients through degree ten are $(7,2,9,6,3,2,1,7,7,0,7)$; the target coefficients are $(8,0,9,4,2,6,8,4,1,1,0)$.

Write $C_j$ for the row of canonical coefficients at $\tau^j d\tau$, restricted to the 48 monomials in the Jacobian-quotient basis of target_cartier.py. Its positive exponent 2 in the covering variable represents the weighted $u^0$ sector, and exponent 4 represents $u^1$. A common pullback factor of two cancels. For the prime-field series, ordinary curve Cartier selects coefficient $11j+10$. Direct comparison gives
$$
 C_{11j+10}=C_j V,\qquad 0\le j\le4,
$$
where $V$ is the independently constructed pole-two matrix. These are 240 scalar equalities. As a secondary consistency check, imposing the same equations with an unknown scalar series multiplying the obstruction forms uniquely recovers all 55 coefficients of the independently computed unit after its constant is fixed to seven. This comparison tests finite jet data in characteristic eleven; it supplies neither the full moving-fiber trace nor the rational correction.

Adding --full uses smooth_sweep.py and python-flint to continue and check the fixed contact to the precision needed for 583 canonical-form coefficients. The determinant of the 48-by-48 matrix with rows $C_0,\ldots,C_{47}$ is 6 modulo 11. Multiplying its inverse by the matrix with rows $C_{10},C_{21},\ldots,C_{527}$ reconstructs an operator using only curve jets in the specified quotient basis. All 2,304 entries agree with the independently computed pole-two matrix. Five further output rows, $j=48,\ldots,52$, agree on every basis vector through input degree 582. They were withheld from the reconstruction. The high-order calculation uses the direct canonical residue throughout and does not fit a normalization from $V$.
