# Eight equations on an open of the marked moving fiber

For fixed ratios $(r,s)$, normalize the mark on the split Segre quadric to $(w,z)=(1,1)$. A sequence of finite algebra eliminations gives eight determinant equations in eight remaining parameters on an explicit open. At the known contact modulo 11 the entire affine one-parameter slice used in the last elimination has exactly one geometric solution of length one. The degree of the full moving fiber and the rational current remain unknown.

## Coordinates and the two axis octics

Order the two roots of each physical binary quadric block. Choose their linear forms to have value one at the mark, assuming the mark misses their zero loci. In Segre coordinates these four forms are $1,wz,w,z$. Write
$$
a=x+(1-x)wz,\quad b=y+(r-y)wz,\quad
c=\alpha(vw+(1-v)z),\quad d=\alpha(jw+(s-j)z),\quad Z=\alpha^2 .
$$
The two binary changes are invertible when $(rx-y)(sv-j)\ne0$. With $P(Z)=H(1,r,\alpha,s\alpha)$, use an even biquadratic $\widehat Q$ of value one at the mark. Its four free coefficients have indices $02,11,20,22$. Put $F=H(a,b,c,d)-P(Z)\widehat Q^3$. The 15 free coefficients of a $(3,3)$ section $h$ satisfying $h(1,1)=0$ must give $F=h(w,z)h(-w,-z)$. Any 24 of the 25 even coefficient equations suffice because both sides vanish at the mark. The system is square on $ZP(Z)(rx-y)(sv-j)\ne0$. Its Jacobian determinant is 8 at the seed modulo 11. To compare to the original intrinsic gauge require $N=x(r-y)+(1-x)y\ne0$ and $(1-b_0)(1-2b_0)(1-3b_0)\ne0$, with $b_0=x(1-x)y(r-y)/N^2$. On the free overlap, orderings add a factor four, while lifting the normalized descriptions restores the choices of $\alpha$, the cube root of $P(Z)$ and the sign of $h$. Component-specific normalization still needs the actual stabilizers. [24-variable equations and seed check](marked_segre.py)

Assume $F_{00}\ne0$ and divide by it, normalizing the constant coefficient of $h$ to one. For an axis cubic $1+Vt+Yt^2+Et^3$ use $T=V+E$. Let $g_i$ be the normalized coefficients on that axis and put
$$L=-g_6,\qquad A=(T^2+L+g_2)/2,\qquad D=2T(A+1),\qquad C=A^2+T^2L+2L-g_4.$$
The monic octic $16(C^2-LD^2)=0$ recovers the coefficients by $E=C/D$, $V=T-E$, $Y=A-TE$ when $D$ is a unit. Indeed $E^2=-g_6$, $2Y-V^2=g_2$ and $Y^2-2VE=g_4$. Perform this calculation on both axes. Solve in increasing $w$-row order for the three remaining coefficients per row using $E_z(E_z-V_zY_z)\ne0$. There remain eight upper-row norm equations and the marking equation. The omitted coefficient at $(6,6)$ follows by evaluation at the mark. This gives eleven equations in eleven unknowns. At the four ordered seed lifts their Jacobian determinants are $5,6,10,10$ modulo 11; all 49 norm coefficients are checked. [Axis and row reconstruction](marked_axis.py)

## The nine-variable equations

Over the nine geometry parameters form the rank-64 tensor product $B$ of the two monic octic algebras. On the base open where the discriminants and norms of the reconstruction denominators are units, this is finite étale. Let $R_1,\ldots,R_9$ be the nine reconstructed residuals, let $U=\sum R_i$ and write $M_f$ for multiplication by $f$. Use
$$\det M_U=0,\qquad [t]\det(M_U+tM_{R_i})=0\quad(1\le i\le8).$$
Require $[t]\det(M_U+tI)$ to be a unit. After étale splitting, the determinant is a product over the 64 sheets. Exactly one factor vanishes and the product of the others is a unit there. Modulo the first equation the other eight equations are their corresponding residuals times that unit. This identifies the local schemes and recovers the selected value of any $f$ as $\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{adj}(M_U)M_f)/\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{adj}(M_U))$.

At $(r,s)=(4,2)$ the seed ordering with geometry $(6,3,2,3,3,4,7,10,8)$ has full octic degrees, $\operatorname{rank}(M_U)=63$ and $\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{adj}(M_U))=10$ modulo 11. The nine-equation Jacobian computed by differences modulo 121 has determinant 8 modulo 11. Independent fixed-geometry quotients by all the residuals have length one at each ordering on the allowed axis open. At two orderings the localized degrees are $(6,8)$ and $(8,6)$, so the full rank-64 determinant calculation is asserted only where its additional units hold. ## Elimination of one more coefficient

Vary $\widehat Q$ by $\lambda wz(1-wz)$. Both axes, the value at the mark and $F_{00}$ stay fixed, leaving base parameters $x,y,v,j,Z,Q_{02},Q_{20},Q_{11}+Q_{22}$. At the seed use $Q_{11}=7+\lambda$. Each $w$-row $i$ of $F$ has $\lambda$-degree at most $\min(i,3)$, since a term of degree $e$ in $\lambda$ contains $w^e$. Induction over the constant row-solve matrices bounds the degrees of the reconstructed rows by $i$. Thus the eight upper residuals have degree at most six and the marking residual has degree at most three.

On the seed slice both axis octics split into eight simple roots over $\mathbf F_{121}$. Seven evaluations determine each residual polynomial in $\lambda$ and the four remaining prime-field inputs verify the interpolation. Taking polynomial gcds for all 64 axis pairs gives 63 unit gcds and the single gcd $\lambda$ at $(T_w,T_z)=(6,9)$. This checks uniqueness and multiplicity over the algebraic closure on the whole stated affine slice.

To eliminate $\lambda$ in families, require the leading coefficient of $U$ to be a unit in $B$ and put $B'=B[\lambda]/(U/\operatorname{lc}U)$, of rank 384. On the slice $U$ has degree six at all 64 axis pairs. Put $W=10R_1+R_2+4R_4+3R_5+3R_6+2R_7+R_8$. The determinant of $M_W$ in $B'$ and its first seven directional derivatives in $M_{R_1},\ldots,M_{R_7}$ give eight equations over the eight base geometry parameters. On $\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{adj}(M_W))\ne0$ at a solution, the zero eigenvalue is simple, so $B'$ splits locally into the corresponding rank-one factor and its complement where $W$ is a unit. This follows from the coprime factors of its characteristic polynomial on the zero locus, and does not require separability of every other root. The equations recover each residual on the rank-one summand, and $R_8$ then follows from $W=0$. The adjugate formula also recovers $\lambda$ and both axis coordinates. At the actual seed the multiplication rank is 383 and the adjugate trace is 7 modulo 11. The checker computes these through the 64 smaller quotient algebras.
