# The coupled core has a non-supersingular summand at inert primes

Let $T$ be the smooth weighted threefold $w^3=-H(a,b,c,d)$ and $\sigma$ the sign change of $c,d$. Point counts yield the following geometric Frobenius traces on the negative eigenspace in $H^3(T)$.

| $p$ | $\operatorname{Tr}(F_p^2)$ | $\operatorname{Tr}(F_p^2)/p^2$ |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 11 | −4,356 | −36 |
| 17 | 4,624 | 16 |
| 23 | −25,392 | −48 |

All three primes are good. For each prime the matrix with rows $x^m\partial_j H$, $|m|=12$, spans all 1,140 monomials of degree 17. The Jacobian ideal has no common projective zero. The weighted vertex lies outside $T$, and the partial derivative $3w^2$ then implies smoothness. [Jacobian check](good_reduction.py)

Take $q=p^2$, $k=5/7\bmod p$, and the quadratic character $\eta$ on $\mathbf F_q^\times$. Let $(a,b),(c,d)$ independently range over $(1,x)$ for $x\in\mathbf F_q$ and $(0,1)$. Write $f= a^6+b^6+k a^5b+2kab^5$, $g=c^6+d^6+kc^5d+3kcd^5$. In this paragraph $a,b,c,d$ denote the chosen line representatives. Put

$$P(z)=f+kabcd(a^2z+2c^2z^2)+gz^3,\qquad n(y)=\#\{w\in\mathbf F_q:w^3=-y\}.$$

The anti-invariant trace is

$$-\sum_{(a,b),(c,d)}\ \sum_{z\in\mathbf F_q^\times}\eta(z)\,n(P(z)).$$

Indeed, the $\sigma$-twisted and ordinary point counts differ by twice the trace in degree three. Outside the middle cohomology, weak Lefschetz and duality give only sign-invariant ambient classes. In either count the loci with one coordinate pair zero contribute equally. Where both pairs are present, the variable $z$ arises as the square of their nonzero relative projective scale. Twisting by $\sigma$ interchanges squares and nonsquares, producing the displayed sign and multiplicity. Since $-1$ is a cube in $\mathbf F_q$, direct cubic-character counting gives $n$. The script also prints the two nontrivial cubic twists as cross-checks. [Point-count computation](inert_trace.py)

If all eigenvalues on this sector came from Tate twists of elliptic curves with complex multiplication by $\mathbf Q(\zeta_3)$, they would all have slope $3/2$ at each listed inert prime. Indeed the CM elliptic curve has supersingular reduction, and this slope is invariant under extension of the residue field. Every eigenvalue of $F_p^2$ would then have valuation three, forcing its rational integer trace to be divisible by $p^3$. Every row violates this divisibility. This certifies a summand outside the isotypic part generated by these CM elliptic curves for the exact coupled core. The aggregate trace does not locate the prescribed class $a^3c^3$ within this decomposition.

Run `python3 good_reduction.py 11 17 23` and `python3 inert_trace.py 11 17 23` with NumPy installed.
